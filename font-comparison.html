About me

Partnerships by day.
Mountains by weekend.

I grew up in France with a simple conviction: the best things happen when you leave the map behind. That idea led me across five continents, up four-thousanders in the Alps, through the ancient pilgrimage trails of Japan, and along coastlines I still can't quite believe are real.

Today I build partnerships at Google Maps in Tokyo — connecting the platform to the places and people that make the world worth exploring. When I'm not at the desk, I'm on a bike, on a ridge, or chasing wind on a kiteboard somewhere warmer than my office.
