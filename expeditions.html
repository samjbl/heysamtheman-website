June 2014

O'Henro
Pilgrimage

Shikoku, Japan88 Buddhist temples3 weeks on foot

Three weeks walking temple to temple around the island of Shikoku. The O'Henro pilgrimage visits 88 temples. You don't complete it in three weeks — but you understand what it asks of you.

My first time properly living in Japan, before I actually moved there. The country got under my skin and never left.

October 2016

Paragliding
License

Another way through mountainsThis time, above them

Got my paragliding license. Learning to read thermals, launch in crosswinds, and land exactly where you intend to. The sky feels very different once you've had to study it seriously.

Still fly when the conditions line up. There's nothing like a mountain that you've also soared above.
