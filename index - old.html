Hi, I'm Sam.

One life.
Two speeds.
No off-season.

I grew up in Montreal with one conviction: the world is better explored on foot. At 23, I walked out of Le Puy-en-Velay and didn't stop until Santiago de Compostela, two months later, without really training for it. I expected to find myself. Instead, I found a habit I still can't shake.

Since then I've stood on the summit of Mont Blanc, turned back twice on Aconcagua and the Matterhorn, cycled the length of Japan from Kagoshima to Tokyo, walked 1,200km around the island of Shikoku, kitesurfed off the Philippines, and skied the backcountry of Hokkaido when the rest of Tokyo was at brunch.

In between all of that, I've built a career in partnerships: Dublin, Singapore, Manila, and now Tokyo, where I lead partnerships at Google. The mountains don't care about your inbox. That's still the whole point.

For brand partners

Looking for an ambassador,
not a post.

Not pitching a single trip. Looking for an ongoing relationship: gear used across three disciplines and four seasons, documented honestly, including the days it doesn't go to plan.

What I bring

  • Cross-discipline use. Climbing, bikepacking, and ski touring gear all get real field testing, year-round, in real conditions.
  • A decade of consistency. Thirteen years of self-funded adventures before asking a brand for anything. This is not a new hobby.
  • Honest documentation. The turn-backs on Aconcagua and the Matterhorn are on this site, right next to the summits. That's the whole idea.
  • A base in Japan. Direct access to the Japanese outdoor market and some of the best backcountry in Asia.
  • Professional credibility. My day job is partnerships at Google. I know what a good brand relationship looks like from both sides.

What I'm asking for

  • A real relationship, not a one-off. Gear support tied to a season or a year, not a single post.
  • Product that matches the objective. Alpine gear for summer, bikepacking kit for the road, touring setups for winter.
  • Honest content. Field use, real conditions, no manufactured outcomes.
  • A conversation first. Happy to start small and let the relationship prove itself before going long-term.
