How we partner together

Sponsorship

Back an adventure, or a whole season.

Support my gear, adventures, travels, permits, teams. In return your brand becomes the main sponsor, is named on the expedition and carried through everything I publish about it.

Get in touch
  • Named support on a specific expedition or a full season
  • Presence across pre-trip, on-route and post-trip content
  • A written debrief when I get back, whatever the outcome
  • Scope and deliverables agreed in writing before departure

Gear testing

Send me a kit. And your best gear to try.

Let me put your equipment to the test. I'll write up what worked, what failed. I'll praise the quality of your gear where I see it. And redirect to brand and ecommerce websites.

Get in touch
  • Alpinism and high altitude, up to 6,500 m
  • Backcountry skiing in Japan and the Alps
  • Multi-week trekking and long-distance cycling
  • Structured written report with photo evidence

Collaborations

Content, campaigns and talks.

I'll shoot content for you that is ready for social media. I also speak and do talks about my expeditions and how you can apply the lessons I've learned to the corporate world. My work at Google puts adventures into context.

Get in touch
  • Stills and video shot on location to a brief
  • Long-form written accounts and campaign copy
  • Keynotes and team sessions, in English or French
  • Usage rights agreed up front
