🚀 My life dreams

Forty-nine things I want to have done in this life. Some are the reason I keep training. Some just need a free month and a bit of nerve.

Done

  • 🏔️ Mont Blanc, 4,808 m A few weeks First time in crampons on the Mer de Glace. Five days later, the summit at dawn. September 2015
  • 🪂 Paragliding licence, then solo on my own glider A few months A flight over Lake Annecy at golden hour is now a normal evening. Done
  • 🏍️ Motorbike licence A few weeks Passed in Japan. The Vietnam trip in 2013 was on a bike I had learned to ride three days before. Japan, 2023
  • 🌋 Kilimanjaro, 5,895 m A few weeks Uhuru Peak at sunrise. First of the Seven Summits. January 2026
🔥

Under way

  • 🗺️ The Seven Summits Years Kilimanjaro is in the bag. Aconcagua stopped me at 6,500 m in 2025 and is owed a second go. 1 of 7
  • ⛰️ All 82 Alpine four-thousanders Years Mont Blanc, Breithorn, Weissmies and the Matterhorn so far. A lifetime project, and that is the point. 4 of 82

Still to come

How long each one takes

  • 🏔️ Everest, 8,849 m Years The one everyone asks about. It comes after the 6,000s and 7,000s, not before.
  • 🏔️ Annapurna, 8,091 m Years The first 8,000 m peak ever climbed, and still one of the hardest.
  • 🧊 Lenin Peak, 7,134 m A few weeks The classic first 7,000er, on the Kyrgyz–Tajik border. Altitude with no technical excuse.
  • 🇳🇵 Two months in Nepal, climbing 6,000 m peaks A few months Not a trek. Island Peak, Lobuche, Mera, one after the other, with time to acclimatise properly.
  • 🥾 Cross the Pyrenees on the GR10 A few months Atlantic to Mediterranean on foot. About 900 km and 50,000 m of climbing.
  • 🛖 A season working in a mountain refuge A few months Carry, cook, wake the climbers at 3 am. See the mountain from the other side of the counter.
  • 🐎 Cross Mongolia on horseback A few weeks I have been to Mongolia. Next time, the steppe the way the nomads cross it.
  • 🚐 The Pan-American Highway, end to end A few months Alaska to Tierra del Fuego in a camper van. The Darién Gap is part of the problem to solve.
  • 🚲 Cycle from France to Japan A few months Home to home, the long way. Both places I live, joined by one road.
  • 🚴 Cycle across France A few weeks Corner to corner. A warm-up for the long one, and a way to see my own country slowly.
  • 🥾 Walk the Via Alpina A few months Trieste to Monaco through eight countries. The whole Alpine arc under my feet.
  • 🌲 Walk the Pacific Crest Trail A few months Mexico to Canada, 4,265 km. Five months of walking, which I know I can do.
  • ⛪ Every monastery in France, on foot Years The Camino in 2013 started this. A slower project, in pieces, over years.
  • 🐧 A season in Antarctica on a scientific mission A few months Not a cruise. Join a research station for a season and live there.
  • ⛵ Cross the Atlantic on a sailboat A few weeks Crew on a transat. Three weeks of ocean and no land.
  • 🌍 Sail around the world, starting in France Years The one that needs everything else first: the licence, the miles, the years.
  • 🪂 Fly a paraglider from the summit of Mont Blanc A few days Climb it again, this time with a wing in the pack. Land in Chamonix for lunch.
  • ✈️ Private pilot licence, and keep flying A few months Not just the licence. Fly often enough that it stays a skill and not a certificate.
  • 🪖 Skydiving licence, then 50 jumps A few months Paragliding taught me the air is a place. Now the fast version.
  • 🚤 Motor boat licence A few days Lake Annecy is right there. Also the first step towards the sailing.
  • 🤿 Become a dive master, then a dive instructor A few months From diver to the person others trust underwater.
  • 🦈 Dive with sharks A few days Thresher sharks at Malapascua were a start. Next, the big ones, without a cage.
  • 🔧 Learn plumbing A few months Enough to fit a bathroom myself and never call anyone for a leak again.
  • ⚡ Learn the basics of electricity A few weeks Wire a new home, safely, and understand what is behind the wall.
  • 🩹 Get first-aid certified A few days Overdue for someone who spends this much time far from help.
  • 👨‍🍳 Study cooking for a year and pass the diploma Years A real kitchen, a real exam. The CAP, or the Japanese equivalent.
  • 🥐 Learn bread and croissants in a bakery A few weeks Early mornings with a baker, then make them at home until they are right.
  • 💐 Work in a flower shop A few weeks Learn the craft from people who do it every day. Come out able to make a proper bouquet.
  • 🎙️ Launch a podcast A few months Long conversations with the people whose experience and intelligence I value most. With a running invitation list, and a curious mind.
  • 📖 Write my family's story A few months Interview everyone while they are here to be interviewed. Write it down before it is gone.
  • 🎸 Learn the guitar Years Well enough to play for people, not just for myself.
  • 🎤 Learn to sing properly A few months Lessons, technique, the works. In tune and without apologising first.
  • 🎭 Act in a play again A few months I did theatre once. Get back on a stage with a real company and a real audience.
  • 🗣️ Become a better public speaker A few months Lessons, and the discipline to cut the filler words for good.
  • 🖼️ Learn the art of the Renaissance A few months Enough to walk into the Uffizi and know what I am looking at, and why it mattered.
  • 🎓 A history degree at the Sorbonne Years The proper way, with the exams. Later in life, when there is time to read everything.
  • 🌿 Know the plants and trees around home A few months Every tree and flower on the paths above Annecy, by name, in French and Latin.
  • 🍄 Learn to find mushrooms A few weeks Which ones, where, and when. Come home from a walk with dinner.
  • 🎣 Get good at fly fishing A few weeks Catch fish in the lakes and rivers of the Alps, on purpose rather than by luck.
  • 🔭 Know the night sky A few weeks The constellations and the planets from any bivouac, without an app.
  • 🐄 A season in the alpages A few months A summer with the sheep and the cows and the people who move them. The Alps at walking pace.
  • 🕯️ A silent retreat in a French monastery A few days A Catholic place full of beauty and calm. A week of not talking, and seeing what is left.
  • 📵 A week without a phone, then a month A few weeks Two stages. The week is the test; the month is the point.
