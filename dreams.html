My life dreams

Forty-nine things I want to have done in this life. Some are the reason I keep training. Some just need a free month and a bit of nerve. When one gets done it moves up, and a new one usually takes its place.

Done

  • Mountains Mont Blanc, 4,808 m First time in crampons on the Mer de Glace. Five days later, the summit at dawn. September 2015
  • Water & sky Paragliding licence, then solo on my own glider A flight over Lake Annecy at golden hour is now a normal evening. Done
  • Trades & skills Motorbike licence Passed in Japan. The Vietnam trip in 2013 was on a bike I had learned to ride three days before. Japan, 2023
  • Mountains Kilimanjaro, 5,895 m Uhuru Peak at sunrise. First of the Seven Summits. January 2026

Under way

  • Mountains The Seven Summits Kilimanjaro is in the bag. Aconcagua stopped me at 6,500 m in 2025 and is owed a second go. 1 of 7
  • Mountains All 82 Alpine four-thousanders Mont Blanc, Breithorn, Weissmies and the Matterhorn so far. A lifetime project, and that is the point. 4 of 82

Still to come

  • Mountains Lenin Peak, 7,134 m The classic first 7,000er, on the Kyrgyz–Tajik border. Altitude with no technical excuse.
  • Mountains Two months in Nepal, climbing 6,000 m peaks Not a trek. Island Peak, Lobuche, Mera, one after the other, with time to acclimatise properly.
  • Mountains Cross the Pyrenees on the GR10 Atlantic to Mediterranean on foot. About 900 km and 50,000 m of climbing.
  • Mountains A season working in a mountain refuge Carry, cook, wake the climbers at 3 am. See the mountain from the other side of the counter.

  • Journeys The Pan-American Highway, end to end Alaska to Tierra del Fuego in a camper van. The Darién Gap is part of the problem to solve.
  • Journeys Cycle from France to Japan Home to home, the long way. Both places I live, joined by one road.
  • Journeys Cycle across France Corner to corner. A warm-up for the long one, and a way to see my own country slowly.
  • Journeys Walk the Via Alpina Trieste to Monaco through eight countries. The whole Alpine arc under my feet.
  • Journeys Walk the Pacific Crest Trail Mexico to Canada, 4,265 km. Five months of walking, which I know I can do.
  • Journeys Every monastery in France, on foot The Camino in 2013 started this. A slower project, in pieces, over years.
